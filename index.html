04 / Guest tier

Ten tasks. No questions.

Most apps ask you to sign up before you've seen anything. Tick doesn't. Open it, start typing. Your tasks live in your browser's localStorage — no server, no account, no waiting.

The cap is ten tasks. When you hit eight, a small note appears below the composer. By ten, the composer greys out. You can still edit, check off, and rearrange everything you've already written. You just can't add the eleventh.

We'd rather point you to the paid plan than pretend the free tier does what it can't. Nothing's hidden behind a wall.

Labels are seeded for you on first open — Work, Personal, Errand, Side — because everyone ends up making them anyway. Categories start empty. You make them when you need them. The list starts empty too: no demo tasks to delete, no sample project to ignore.

When you do want more, an optional Clerk account unlocks unlimited tasks, sync across devices, version history, and the calendar timeline on a 30-day horizon instead of seven. The upgrade prompt lives in the composer and the sidebar meter — never a modal, never an interstitial.

05 / Theme

Light, dark, or follow the system.

Pick once in Settings; it sticks across reloads via localStorage. Dark mode uses a warm near-black, not zinc — easier on the eyes at 2am when you're writing tomorrow's list. System mode follows prefers-color-scheme and re-renders without a flash.
